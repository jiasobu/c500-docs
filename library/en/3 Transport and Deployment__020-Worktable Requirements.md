### Worktable Requirements


The machine must be placed on a worktable or installation platform that meets the following requirements, before installation, leveling, and machining operations.

**Load Capacity**

The worktable must have a rated load capacity of at least 250 kg, and must be capable of withstanding the dynamic loads generated during machine operation.

**Stability and Rigidity**

The worktable must have sufficient structural rigidity and stability. It must not wobble, tilt, or show visible deformation.

A heavy cast-iron worktable, welded steel worktable, or another installation platform with sufficient strength and rigidity is recommended. A support platform with insufficient stability or structural strength may amplify machining vibration and affect machining accuracy.

For a mobile worktable with casters, the casters must have reliable locking mechanisms and must remain locked during machine operation.

**Leveling Capability**

The worktable must allow the machine to be leveled, and the platform surface must be flat.

Before installation, it is recommended to check the worktable surface with a level to make sure the base surface is generally level. After installation, the machine must be precisely leveled using its leveling feet.

Using a worktable with adjustable feet or another installation platform featuring leveling functions is recommended.

**Vibration Damping and Noise Reduction**

When the machine is installed on a floor or in another environment where vibration may be transmitted, appropriate vibration damping measures are recommended based on site conditions to reduce the impact of vibration and noise on machine operation and the surrounding environment.

For high-precision machining scenarios, the rigidity and vibration-dampening performance of the installation platform can be further enhanced according to actual requirements.
