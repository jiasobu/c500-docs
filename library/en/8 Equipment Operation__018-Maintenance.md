### Maintenance

Regularly open the drain valve at the bottom of the water tank to remove dirty water and debris, preventing blockages in the water lines and spray head. Drain every 1–2 weeks, depending on usage frequency.  

Clean the inside of the water tank and the spray head regularly. If the spray head becomes blocked, flush with clean water or disassemble for cleaning.  

Refill the water tank with purified water or dedicated coolant as needed based on the machining conditions to maintain the proper liquid level.
