### Account Settings

After entering the Account Settings page, users can log in by scanning a QR code with the mobile App or manually using an email account and password.

Click **Email Login** to open the login window. Enter the complete email address used during registration, then click **Next** to enter the password.

If the email format or password is incorrect, the system will display a prompt message. After successful login, the interface will display the current account avatar and username.

Click **Log Out** to sign out of the current account.
