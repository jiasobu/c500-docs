#### Installation Preparation

Before installation, prepare the following materials and tools: 4th-Axis unit ×1, tailstock center ×1, φ6×20 locating pins ×2, M6×20 hex socket screws ×4, M6×18 hex socket screws ×6, M6 sliding nuts ×4, and a 5 mm hex key.

Ensure the worktable mounting surface is clean and level, and confirm the machine is powered off or in E-Stop status.
