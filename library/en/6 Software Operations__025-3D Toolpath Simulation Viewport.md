#### 3D Toolpath Simulation Viewport

**Basic View**  

Users can rotate the model using mouse drag and zoom with the scroll wheel for multi-angle preview, allowing early detection of overcut or tool collision risks.

**Toolpath Trajectory**  

Different colors are used to identify machining paths, allowing users to verify machining results in advance and reduce the risk of workpiece deformation.

**Bottom Control**  

Click the **play** to view the machining simulation animation in real time.
