### Post-Removal Inspection

After all transport restraints have been removed, arrange and store the removed brackets, handles, and fixing screws properly.

Check the machine body, spindle, and worktable structure. Confirm that no transport restraints remain, no screws are left inside the machine, and there is no compression deformation or structural jamming. Gently push the spindle and worktable by hand to confirm that the structure moves smoothly without hard limits or restraints.
