### Power Requirements

> [!caution] Caution  
> If the machine is moved from a cold environment to a warm environment, do not power it on immediately. It is recommended to let the machine stand for 4 to 6 hours until any condensation has fully evaporated before applying power.

Before connecting the machine to power, make sure the power supply circuit meets the following requirements:

**Table 3-3 Power Requirements** <!-- mdwb:table id=de5c8863d6a2e6d7 -->

| Item                  | Requirement                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Input voltage         | 110 ~ 240 VAC                                                                                    |
| Frequency             | 50 / 60 Hz                                                                                       |
| Total power           | 1.6 kW                                                                                           |
| Power supply circuit  | A dedicated power supply circuit is recommended                                                  |
| Leakage Protection    | Install a residual current device with a rated residual operating current of not less than 30 mA |
| Grounding Requirement | The power outlet must be properly grounded                                                       |

> [!danger] Danger  
> Do not use an ungrounded or poorly grounded power outlet. failure to follow instructions may result in electric shock hazards, machine damage, or control system interference.
