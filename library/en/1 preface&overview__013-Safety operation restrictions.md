### Safety operation restrictions

- It is prohibited to bypass, short-circuit, or modify safety features such as the protective door interlock, Emergency Stop (E-Stop) button, and limit switches without authorization; such actions will invalidate the warranty immediately, and the user shall assume full responsibility for any resulting safety accidents or machine damage.
- It is prohibited to open the protective door, extend any part of the body into the machining chamber, or touch rotating or moving components, including the spindle, chip auger, and motion axes, while the machine is in operation.
- It is prohibited to perform machining operations without appropriate personal protective machine; operators must wear standardized safety goggles, and hearing protection must be worn in high-noise scenarios.
- It is prohibited to perform tool changes, workpiece clamping, or cleaning and maintenance while the machine is running; all such operations must only be performed after the E-Stop button is pressed and the main power supply is disconnected.
- It is prohibited to allow minors to operate the machine, and personnel lacking relevant professional knowledge and operational training are prohibited from operating the machine independently.
- It is prohibited to conduct high-risk machining while the machine is unattended; unattended machining is only permitted after comprehensive program verification, safety shielding, and risk assessments are completed, and all safety functions are fully functional.
- It is hereby specified that tampering with safety devices including protective door and E-Stop shall immediately invalidate the full machine warranty, and the operator shall bear full liability for all resulting accidents.
