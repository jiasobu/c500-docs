#### Outer Packaging Inspection

- Check whether the wooden pallet, carton, and sealing structure are cracked, loose, deformed, or damaged.
- Check the packaging surface for abnormalities such as impact, crushing, moisture, mold, or stains.
- Check whether the sealing, straps, and fixed positions are secure, without loosening or detachment.
- Check whether the packaging labels, machine model, and batch information are correct.

After confirming that the inner packaging protection is normal, remove the outer protective structure of the inner packaging as follows:

1. Untie the packaging straps, remove the top cover of the carton, and remove the carton panels around the machine.
2. Remove the protective EPE foam outside the machine body.
