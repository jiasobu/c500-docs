## Safety Sign Definitions

This manual and the safety labels on the machine use three levels of safety signs to indicate different degrees of risk or operational precautions. Users must read and follow them carefully:



> [!danger] Danger
> Indicates a high-risk situation that may result in personal injury or serious machine damage. Follow the instructions carefully and avoid ignoring or violating these warnings.

> [!caution] Caution  
> Indicates operations that may affect machine performance, machining quality, or normal use. Handle with care to avoid potential issues.

> [!note] Note
> Provides operational advice, supplementary explanations, or information that helps improve operational efficiency.
