### Laser Framing

Laser framing is used to check for processing offset errors in advance, preventing machining failures caused by stock clamping misalignments, and assisting in precise pre-machining positioning calibration.


> [!note] Notice
> The framing feature is intended for positioning previews only and will not cut the workpiece. If contour offsets occur, it is recommended to adjust the workpiece clamping position and re-verify.
