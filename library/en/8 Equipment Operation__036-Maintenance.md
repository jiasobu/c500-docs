### Maintenance

Develop a maintenance schedule for the vise body based on the machine’s operating hours and frequency of use.

**Table 8-3 Maintenance** <!-- mdwb:table id=769a5fbb87d72251 -->

|Operating Condition|Recommended Maintenance Interval|
|---|---|
|More than 10 h of operation per day|Perform vise body maintenance every 7 days|
|Less than 10 h of operation per day, or intermittent use|Perform vise body maintenance every 15–30 days|
|Extended periods of non-use|Perform complete vise body maintenance, then return the vise to its packaging box or seal and store it in a dry environment|

Perform vise body maintenance as follows:

1. Use an air gun or soft brush to remove chips and residual cutting fluid from the vise body, guide surfaces, and gaps around the jaws.
    
2. Apply sufficient anti-rust oil evenly to the inside of the vise body and the guide surfaces, until a small amount of oil flows out. Apply anti-rust oil evenly to the outer surfaces of the vise body and jaws, forming a protective oil film.
