#### Tool Sleeve Installation and Removal Tool

The milling cutter must be installed into the matching tool sleeve before it can be used with this machine. Milling cutters are consumable parts and are prone to breakage or wear during use. They must be replaced regularly. After the milling cutter is installed into the tool sleeve, it must also be installed into a collet of the corresponding diameter before being used with the spindle for cutting.

Milling cutters are available in four diameter specifications: Φ3 mm, Φ3.15 mm, Φ6 mm, and Φ6.35 mm. The tool sleeves and collets are provided in the corresponding four specifications to accommodate different milling cutters. The tool sleeve assembly and disassembly cylinder is compatible with all four specifications.

The tool sleeve assembly and disassembly cylinder is mainly used to press-fit the milling cutter into the tool sleeve or remove the milling cutter from the tool sleeve. It can also be used for collet installation and removal.
