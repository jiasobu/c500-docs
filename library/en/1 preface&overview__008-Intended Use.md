## Intended Use

The main intended use of this equipment is CNC milling processing in a controlled indoor environment, which specifically includes:

- Perform CNC machining processes such as precision milling, drilling, reaming, thread milling, surface engraving, and contour machining on non-metallic materials including engineering plastics, acrylic, wood, epoxy boards, and fiberglass boards, as well as metallic materials such as aluminum alloy, copper, brass, and stainless steel.
- Prototype making of structural parts, hand model production, customized processing of tooling fixtures in the industrial product R&D stage, and small-batch, multi-variety production of precision parts.
- Instructional training, skills development, and science outreach related to CNC technology and mechanical processing.
- Personalized and non-standard precision processing scenarios, including maker creations, art design, custom cultural products, and jewelry processing.
- This machine must not be used in areas requiring explosion protection, for the manufacture of components used with special equipment, or for machining classified military parts. If this machine is used beyond the intended use and applicable scope specified in this manual, the original manufacturer’s warranty will automatically become void. The user shall bear sole responsibility for any resulting safety incidents, regulatory penalties, and other losses.
