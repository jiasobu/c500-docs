### Environmental Requirements

The machine is only suitable for indoor, dry environments without explosion hazards, strongly corrosive gases, or conductive dust. The operating and storage environments must meet the following requirements:

**Table 3-2 Environmental Requirements** <!-- mdwb:table id=98ab757cbcb01042 -->

| Item                  | Requirement                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Operating temperature | 5 ℃ ~ 50 ℃ (41 °F ~ 122 °F)     |
| Storage temperature   | -20 ℃ ~ 50 ℃ (-4 °F ~ 122 °F)   |
| Relative humidity     | 20 % ~ 90 % RH, no condensation |
| Altitude              | ≤ 1829 m (6000 ft)              |

> [!danger] Danger  
> Do not use this machine in flammable or explosive environments, environments with strongly corrosive gases, conductive dust, outdoor rain or moisture exposure, or strong electromagnetic interference.
