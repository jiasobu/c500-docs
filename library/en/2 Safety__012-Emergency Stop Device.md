## E-Stop Device

**Location and Function**

The red E-Stop button is located on the front right side of the machine, to the right of the touchscreen. When pressed, the system immediately cuts off power to the moving components, stops all axis motion, turns off the laser output, and triggers an alarm.

> [!note] Note  
> The E-Stop on the MPG Handwheel can work together with the E-Stop on the main unit. Pressing either E-Stop will trigger the emergency stop function of the machine.

**Use Cases**

1. A mechanical collision is imminent: The tool or spindle is about to collide with the worktable, tool magazine, workholding fixture, workpiece, or other machine components;
2. An abnormal condition occurs during machining: The machine produces abnormal noise, severe vibration, or high-pitched noise, indicating a risk of tool breakage or fixture loosening;
3. Signs of fire or burning are detected: Abnormal sparks, smoke, or a burning odor occurs in the cutting area, indicating a risk of fire or component damage;
4. The workpiece, tool, or fastener becomes unstable: The workpiece, tool, or fastener becomes loose, is ejected, or slips out of position;
5. A foreign object enters the machine motion area: A foreign object accidentally falls into the machine motion area and may become caught in a moving axis or lead screw;
6. Personnel safety is at risk: A person’s body or clothing is at risk of being caught, drawn into, or pinched by moving parts;
7. Machine motion becomes uncontrollable: An axis continues moving without responding to program commands, or an abnormal automatic tool change operation cannot be stopped;
8. A major auxiliary system failure occurs: The MQL lubrication, chip evacuation, or another auxiliary system experiences a serious failure, and continued machining may severely damage the tool or workpiece;
9. A fluid line leaks: A water or pneumatic line develops a major leak that creates a safety hazard;
10. Another hazardous condition occurs: The machine cannot be stopped through normal software controls, and continued operation may result in machine damage or a safety incident.

**Reset Procedure**

1. Troubleshoot and clear the machine fault.
2. Rotate the E-Stop clockwise to release it.
3. Clear the alarm in the control software and perform a reset.
4. Perform homing again to restore the machine coordinate system before continuing machining.
