## Declaration of Conformity

Official Statement of Shenzhen NestWorks Technology Co., Ltd.

Shenzhen NestWorks Technology Co., Ltd. hereby solemnly declares that the NestWorks C500 machine complies with the EU Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC, Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU, RED Radio machine Directive and RoHS Restriction of Hazardous Substances Directive. Its laser safety conforms to standard EN/IEC 60825-1.

This statement is only valid for unmodified original complete machines operated in accordance with the specifications in the operation manual. This declaration of conformity shall automatically become void once the user carries out unauthorized modifications or replaces parts, and the product will no longer meet EU export compliance requirements.

This product is only designed for indoor desktop non-continuous non-industrial processing scenarios. It fails to meet the compliance standards for heavy-duty industrial machine and shall not be deployed as supporting machine on EU industrial production lines. The purchaser shall independently cooperate to provide complete sets of original manufacturer’s documents for customs inspections and market surveillance audits when exporting to the EU market.

All losses including EU regulatory penalties, product recalls and cargo detention arising from unauthorized user modifications or illegal cross-border use shall be borne entirely by the purchaser.
