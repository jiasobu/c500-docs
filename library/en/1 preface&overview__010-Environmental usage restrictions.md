### Environmental usage restrictions

 This machine is only suitable for use in indoor dry environments, free from explosion risks, strong corrosive gases, and conductive dust. If the equipment is operated in any of the aforementioned prohibited environments, permanent precision drift, hardware corrosion and damage shall not be covered under warranty, and the user shall bear full liability for any risks of electric leakage or fire that occur. The environmental parameters for equipment operation must strictly adhere to the following restrictions:

- Operating Temperature: 5°C to 50°C (41°F to 122°F);
- Storage Temperature: -20°C to 50°C (-4°F to 122°F);
- Relative Humidity: 20% to 90%, non-condensing;
- Altitude: no more than 1829 meters (6000 feet). 

It is prohibited to use the machine in flammable, explosive, or excessively dusty environments, outdoor rainy or humid conditions, and areas with strong electromagnetic interference.
