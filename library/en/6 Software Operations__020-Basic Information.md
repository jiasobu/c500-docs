#### Basic Information

**Material Settings**

Click **Material** to open the selection window and choose your target material. Additionally, when the machine is connected, users can scan the QR code on the material to automatically populate the material type and dimensions.

**Alignment Settings**

Users can adjust the relative position of the model within the stock using vertical and horizontal alignments:

* **Vertical Alignment**: Adjusts the vertical positioning to the top face, center layer, or bottom face.

* **Horizontal Alignment**: Adjusts the horizontal positioning to the left, center, or right.

**Model Sizing**

Click the dimension input box to open the model size settings panel. Users can modify the scale of the model by entering or adjusting the length (**X**), width (**Y**), and height (**Z**) values.
