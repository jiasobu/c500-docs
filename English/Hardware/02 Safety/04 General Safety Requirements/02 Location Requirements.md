### Location Requirements

- The machine net weight is 130 kg and shall be placed on a flat, level, and stable machine support table with sufficient load\-bearing capacity. The rated load capacity of the machine support table shall be no less than 250 kg, and the floor load\-bearing capacity of the installation area shall be no less than 400 kg/m². Do not stack two or more machines directly on top of each other for installation or storage. 

- The machine support table must provide good vibration resistance and dynamic stability. It must not wobble, tilt, or show visible deformation\. Do not use a mobile machine support table with casters unless the casters have reliable brakes and the table remains stable when locked.

- The flatness deviation of the table surface must not exceed 1 mm/m. Use a level to check and adjust it. After installation, inspect the machine's position and adjust the feet as needed to ensure that the machine is level.

- The machine does not need to be anchored.

- Keep the work area clean, dry, well lit, and properly ventilated. Dust, smoke, oil mist, or other volatile substances generated during machining should be removed promptly. Use ventilation, dust collection, or air purification measures as needed to prevent indoor accumulation. 

- The machine must be shielded from direct sunlight, rain, condensation, heat sources, and other environmental factors that could affect its normal operation. 

- Do not place combustible materials such as cardboard, wood chips, cloth, plastics, or other debris around the machine.

- During operation, keep children, pets, and other unauthorized personnel away from the machining area and the machine. 

- Allow sufficient space around the machine for operation, maintenance, and cable routing.
