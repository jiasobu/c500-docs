### Power Disconnection Requirements


Follow the steps below to safely disconnect the machine from the power supply in an emergency:

1. Press the E-stop button.
2. Turn off the main power switch by setting the 3-in-1 switch to the "0" position.
3. Wait at least 5 minutes for the internal energy-storing components to discharge.
4. When it is safe to do so, disconnect the machine from the power supply by unplugging the power cord. Qualified electrical personnel must use appropriate test equipment to verify the absence of hazardous voltage.

> [!danger] Danger
> The E-stop button does not disconnect the machine from the power supply. Residual voltage may remain inside the machine even after the power supply has been disconnected. Do not touch internal electrical components until the absence of hazardous voltage has been verified.
