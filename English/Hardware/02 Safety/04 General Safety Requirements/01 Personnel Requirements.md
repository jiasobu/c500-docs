### Personnel Requirements

This machine must only be operated independently by adults who are familiar with the applicable safety regulations. Minors are prohibited from operating the machine. If minors enter the work area for observation, they must be accompanied and closely supervised at all times by a qualified adult, and must not touch the machine, cutting tools, switches, or workpieces.


- Wear safety goggles during operation.
- Do not wear gloves when approaching rotating components, such as the spindle or cutting tools, until they stop completely. Once the machine has stopped and the prescribed measures to prevent unexpected start-up have been taken, wear gloves appropriate to the hazards when loading or unloading workpieces, changing tools, or clearing chips.
- Individuals with long hair must tie it up or take other protective measures to prevent it from being entangled in moving parts.
- Do not wear loose clothing, scarves, ties, jewelry, or other items that may be caught by the machine.
- Maintain full concentration during operation. Operating the machine under the influence of alcohol, fatigue, illness, or medications that may impair judgment or responsiveness is strictly prohibited.
- Smoking, eating, drinking, or any other behavior that may affect safety is strictly prohibited within the work area.
