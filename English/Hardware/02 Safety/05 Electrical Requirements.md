## Electrical Requirements


> [!danger] DANGER
> All accessories do not support hot plugging. Do not install, remove, connect, or disconnect any accessory while the machine is powered on. Ensure the machine is powered off before operation. Failure to follow these instructions can result in personal injury or equipment damage.


- The power supply must be connected to a single-phase AC outlet with effective grounding. It is prohibited to use ungrounded outlets or to remove the grounding pin.
- Avoid stepping on, squeezing, or cutting the cables; ensure all cables move smoothly without being pulled or stretched.
- It is prohibited for non-professionals to disassemble the machine. Power must be turned off and the plug removed before any electrical maintenance.
- It is prohibited to remove the machine casing or touch electrical components while the power is on to prevent electric shock.
- The inverter and the driver contain residual high voltage for 5 minutes after the power is disconnected. It is prohibited to open the cover during this period. Only certified factory technicians are permitted to open the cover for maintenance. Any electric shock or hardware damage resulting from unauthorized cover opening shall void the warranty.
