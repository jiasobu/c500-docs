## General Safety Guidelines

Before operating this machine, please read and understand all safety instructions and operating procedures. Ensure full compliance with safety standards, and verify that the machine is properly assembled and in good working order.

Please follow these principles:

- Check the machine for damage, looseness, or abnormalities before power-on. Don't use machine with any breakdowns or damage.
- Keep the work area clean and clear of debris. Ensure the worktable is stable and level.
- Don't disassemble or modify the machine structure without authorization. Don't modify or decompile the system firmware or software.
- Monitor the machine’s operating status throughout the process and avoid leaving the work area.
- Clean up chips and residue promptly to prevent fire hazards.
