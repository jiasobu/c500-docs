### MPG Handwheel Connection (Optional)  
  
The MPG handwheel is used for precise manual control of the machine and can assist with tool setting, workpiece positioning, and other operations.

> [!danger] DANGER
> The MPG handwheel does not support hot plugging. Ensure the machine is powered off before connecting or disconnecting the MPG handwheel. Do not plug or unplug the MPG handwheel while the machine is powered on.

**Installation Steps**

1. Align the plug at the end of the MPG handwheel coiled cable with the handwheel port on the machine. Ensure that the red dot on the plug is aligned with the red dot on the socket, and gently insert the plug until it is fully seated.

![](assets/plug-2c55e3c0e7b9.png){width=10cm}

2. Start the machine and enter the control system. Switch to MPG handwheel control mode, then test manual movement of the three axes in sequence to confirm that the movement direction and control response are normal.

![](assets/Handwheel-4914cbe9cbd0.png){width=14cm}

**Software Instructions**

- In MPG handwheel control mode, the X, Y, and Z axes can be precisely controlled by rotating the MPG handwheel. This mode is used for tool setting, workpiece positioning, and other fine adjustment operations.
- The MPG handwheel is equipped with selector knobs for selecting the controlled axis and movement increment. The selected axis can be moved by rotating the dial. The rotation speed determines the movement speed, and the rotation angle determines the movement distance.
- After use, switch back to automatic control mode. The MPG handwheel will stop working and will not affect normal automatic machining.

**Disconnecting the Handwheel**

1. Ensure that the machine has stopped running and is in standby mode.

2. Hold the anti-slip grip area at the front of the plug and pull it straight backward smoothly to disconnect the plug from the aviation socket.
