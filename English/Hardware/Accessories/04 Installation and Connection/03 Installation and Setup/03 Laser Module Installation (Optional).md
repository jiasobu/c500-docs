### Laser Module Installation (Optional)

This machine supports an optional 5 W laser module for laser engraving. It is suitable for processing materials such as solid wood, bamboo, leather, cardboard, and coated metal.

**Installation Steps**

1. Hold both sides of the laser module with both hands. Press and hold the quick-release button on the front of the laser module with your thumb.

![](assets/Installation_Steps_01-ff04cb40c9cb.png){width=12cm}

2. While holding the quick-release button, align the laser dovetail slot with the spindle laser mounting rail. Slide the laser module along the mounting rail until it is fully seated, then release the quick-release button.

![](assets/Installation_Steps_02-08433f1d4f22.png){width=12cm}

3. Gently shake the laser module to ensure it is securely installed.

![](assets/Installation_Steps_03-35eaefa5d4f4.png){width=12cm}


4. Remove the connection cable from the left side of the laser module. Align the connector with the socket on the left side of the spindle, then insert it after confirming proper alignment.

![](assets/Installation_Steps_04-12a66c43f0b9.png){width=12cm}



**Removal Steps**

1. Hold the front section of the aviation connector on the left side of the spindle, then pull out the laser module connector.

![](assets/Removal_Steps_01-5a86bd7255ae.jpg){width=12cm}

2. Hold both sides of the laser module with both hands. Press and hold the quick-release button on the front of the laser module with your thumb, then slide the laser module forward along the mounting rail to remove it.

![](assets/Removal_Steps_02-b2ec2be91187.jpg){width=12cm}

**Laser Processing Setup**


Power on the machine and enter the system interface. Set parameters such as laser power, focal length, and engraving speed, according to the material and processing requirements.

Manually adjust the laser module position to align the laser focus with the workpiece surface. After adjustment, perform a small-area test engraving to check engraving clarity and depth before starting formal machining.


> [!danger] Danger
> - The laser module generates high temperatures and high-intensity laser radiation during operation. Do not look directly into the laser emission port. Always wear dedicated laser safety goggles during operation.
> - When processing flammable or explosive materials, take fire and explosion prevention measures in advance and prepare fire extinguishing machine on site.


> [!caution] Caution
>
> - Before use, ensure the laser module is mounted securely without looseness or deviation to prevent machine vibration from affecting engraving precision.
> - Clean the surface of the focusing lens regularly using a dedicated cleaning cloth. Do not scrape the lens with hard objects to avoid damage.
> - If the laser module fails to start, shows abnormal laser output, or has poor electrical contact, stop the machine and disconnect the power immediately. Contact professional personnel for maintenance.

> [!note] Notice
> - Turn off the machine power when the laser module is not in use for an extended period. Avoid impact or dropping during transportation.
