#### Testing

1. After the vise is powered on, test whether the buttons function properly. Press the release and clamp buttons separately, and confirm that the vise operates normally.

2. After confirming that the vise operates normally, press the clamp button until the vise is fully clamped. Wait for 2 s until the motor enters stall protection mode. Pressing the clamp button again should produce no movement. Press the release button to re-enable the vise and exit protection mode. Repeat the same test after pressing the release button until the vise reaches its maximum travel.
