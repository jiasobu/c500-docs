### PC Operation



After the software is connected to the machine, if the 4th-Axis module is connected and recognized, users can perform A-axis homing separately in the control console.



In 4th-Axis mode, users can import 3D models for rotary machining. The 4th-Axis mode differs slightly from the 3-axis mode. In addition to setting basic stock dimensions, users can switch the Stock type between Round and Square, and set rotary machining parameters.

Common 4th-Axis toolpath settings include Material Size(mm), Stock & Model Alignment Settings, and Toolpath Strategy. After setting the required parameters, click **Create Toolpath** and view the machining file information on the Preview page.
