#### Maintenance

A maintenance schedule for the 4th-Axis chuck is recommended based on machine usage frequency.

**Table 8-2 Maintenance** <!-- mdwb:table id=f68fe251aad0ba84 -->

| Usage Condition                                                    | Maintenance Interval                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Daily operating time exceeds 10 h/day                              | Perform chuck maintenance every 7 days                                                    |
| Daily operating time is less than 10 h/day, or used intermittently | Perform chuck maintenance every 15–30 days                                                |
| Long-term storage                                                  | Complete a full maintenance procedure, then seal and store the chuck in the packaging box |


Chuck maintenance procedures:

1. Use an air gun or soft brush to remove chips and coolant residue from the chuck body, guide surfaces, and jaw gaps.  
2. Spray anti-rust oil evenly into the interior of the chuck and onto the guideway surfaces until a small amount of oil overflows. Ensure the surfaces of the chuck and jaws are fully coated for rust prevention.
3. Inject grease through the chuck lubrication port until fresh grease overflows from the inner jaw holes.
