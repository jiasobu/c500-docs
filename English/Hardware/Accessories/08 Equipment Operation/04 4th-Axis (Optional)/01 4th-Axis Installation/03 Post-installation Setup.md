#### Post-installation Setup

> [!caution] Caution  
> The 4th-Axis hardware does not support hot plugging. Do not plug or unplug the aviation connector while the machine is powered on.

1. Start the machine and enter the control system. Under normal conditions, the machine automatically detects the 4th-Axis during the power-on self-check, and the 4th-Axis control function will appear on the NestPad interface. If the function is not displayed, check whether the wiring and 4th-Axis firmware are normal.


2. Perform a homing operation. The machine first completes homing for the X, Y, and Z axes, then performs homing for the A axis. During A-axis homing, the A axis first rotates quickly in one direction and stops after triggering the limit switch. then it rotates in the other direction, and slowly moves back toward the home direction until the limit switch is triggered again, completing the homing process. If the A axis continues rotating in the same direction, then stops and triggers an alarm, check whether the A-axis limit switch is normal.


> [!caution] Caution
> 
> - The 4th-Axis must be installed using the corresponding threaded holes and locating pin holes on the worktable or adapter plate.
>     
> - Do not add extra nuts in the T-slots of the worktable to secure the 4th-Axis. Incorrect installation positions may cause collision risks and result in machine damage.
>     
> - Do not rinse the inside of the chuck directly with water, as this may reduce the effectiveness of internal lubrication and rust prevention. Clean the chuck and apply rust prevention after use.
>     
> - Do not use a self-centering chuck to clamp irregular workpieces. Uneven force on the jaws may cause deformation or jamming.
>
