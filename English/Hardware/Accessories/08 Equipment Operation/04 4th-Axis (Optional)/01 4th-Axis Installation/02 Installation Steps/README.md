#### Installation Steps

1. Place the tailstock on the base plate with the live center pointing toward the 4th axis chuck. Pre-fasten and secure it using 4 pcs of M6×20 hexagon socket head cap screws and 4 pcs of M6 T-slot nuts.

![](../../../../../../../assets/4th-Axis_step1-7a9912b2d78a.png){width=10cm}

2. Lightly press the φ6×20 locating pins into the corresponding locating holes on the worktable. Align the locating holes on the 4th-Axis base plate with the locating pins to determine the installation position of the 4th-Axis module. The 4th-Axis threaded hole markings on the worktable can be used as positioning references.  


![](../../../../../../../assets/4th-Axis_step2-15434539981b.png){width=10cm}

3. Insert 6 pcs of M6×18 hex socket screws through the mounting holes on the 4th-Axis base plate and tighten them using a 5 mm hex key to complete structural installation.  

![](../../../../../../../assets/4th-Axis_step3-fc14f2663693.png){width=10cm}

4. Locate the 4th-Axis and vise aviation connector socket inside the machining chamber. Align the keying positions of the male and female aviation connectors, connect the 4th-Axis aviation connector to the machine connector, and manually tighten the locking nut to complete the signal connection.

5. Power on the machine or release the **E-Stop** , close the protective door, and click **Home Reset**. After the machine completes the homing process, gently rotate the 4th-Axis chuck by hand to verify that the chuck is securely mounted and free of noticeable rotational backlash. Once confirmed, installation is complete.
