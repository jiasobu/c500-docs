## Intended Users

- Individual makers, DIY enthusiasts, independent designers, and prototypers. Used for bringing designs to life, personalized parts machining, and creative work.
- Teachers and students in majors such as mechanical manufacturing, CNC technology, intelligent manufacturing, and mold design at universities, vocational colleges, and technical schools, for practical training, project development, and competition preparation.
- Product R&D (Research and Development) departments of enterprises, innovation and entrepreneurship teams, and research institutions, for prototyping new product structural components, small-batch production of precision parts, and customized machining of tooling fixtures.
- Professional technicians in fields such as PCB (Printed Circuit Board) manufacturing, precision molds, jewelry processing, and medical device components—used for high-precision and small-batch parts machining scenarios.
- This machine is prohibited from being used to manufacture medical implants or parts related to life support systems. It shall not be sold or delivered to untrained minors for independent use. Training institutions purchasing this machine must establish complete practical operation training and supervision systems. Any accidents arising from failure to fulfill supervision responsibilities shall be borne solely by the institution.
