# Overview



The NestWorks C500 is a high-precision CNC milling machine developed for desktop applications, integrating core configurations such as a closed-loop motion control system, a high-speed and high-precision spindle, an automatic tool changer, and a safety interlock system. The machine combines industrial-grade machining accuracy with desktop-level ease of use. It is compatible with standard ISO G-code and mainstream CAM software, and supports a variety of machining processes, including milling, drilling, boring, thread milling, and engraving. The machine is equipped with a 3D probe and supports automatic tool setting and intelligent collision prevention, meeting the needs of both beginner and professional users.
