### Handling Tool Requirements

- Machine handling must utilize forklifts, hydraulic lift trucks, gantry cranes, or other compliant handling machine. The rated load of the handling machine shall not be lower than the total weight of the machine. Handling machine with a rated load of at least 200 kg is recommended.
- When lifting the machine, use suitable lifting tools and rigging. Lifting eye screw holes are provided on the handle adapter seats at the machine base. Before lifting, make sure the lifting eye screws are fully inserted and securely tightened. Keep the slings as vertical as possible to avoid pressing against the protective door. The included angle between the slings must not exceed 60° to prevent additional lateral loads on the lifting eye screws.

- When using forklifts or hydraulic lift trucks, lift from the bottom of the machine base and implement necessary anti-slip and anti-tipping measures.
- During handling, the handles on the machine base may be held for auxiliary support.


> [!danger] Danger  
> The overall center of gravity of this machine is located toward the rear and lower part of the machine. During lifting, the rear side of the machine may naturally sink, while the front side may rise.
> 
> Perform hoisting operations slowly and smoothly, avoiding sudden acceleration, abrupt stops, or violent shaking. When lifting with slings, make sure the slings on both sides have the same length to prevent obvious tilting caused by the rear-biased center of gravity.
> During handling, auxiliary personnel must maintain a safe distance from the machine. Standing directly behind the machine or in the potential direction of tipping is strictly prohibited.

> [!danger] Danger  
> Keep the protective door closed during handling.
> 
> Do not grasp the protective door, door handles, or sheet metal enclosure for leverage during handling. Failure to follow this instruction may result in machine damage or personal injury.
