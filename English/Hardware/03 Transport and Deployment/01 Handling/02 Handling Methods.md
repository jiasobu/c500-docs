### Handling Methods

#### Lifting and Handling

**Preparation**

- Use a gantry crane or jib crane with a rated load of at least 250 kg.
- Use synthetic-fiber lifting slings, each with a rated load of at least 250 kg and a combined rated load of at least 1,000 kg.
- Do not mix slings of different lengths. Do not use slings that are damaged, cut, or aged.

At least two people must work together during handling and lifting. One person operates the lifting equipment and controls its movement and lifting. The other person observes and stabilizes the machine's position, monitors shifts in the center of gravity and collision risks, and signals when the operation must stop.

**Procedure**

1. Clear the lifting route and placement area of obstacles. 

2. Route the slings around both sides of the outer wooden crate and pass them beneath the bottom pallet so that they support the crate and pallet as a whole. Gather the upper ends of the slings and attach them to the lifting hook.

   <div style="position:absolute;top:-14px;left:10px;background:#fff;border:1px solid #ed8b00;border-radius:2px;padding:2px 10px;color:#ed8b00;font-weight:700;line-height:22px;">⚠ Caution</div>
   <div style="padding-top:0px;line-height:1.75;color:#333;"><br>- Do not place the slings over the crate lid, weak side panels, or latches. Install corner protectors wherever the slings contact the crate edges.</div>
   </div>

3. Tighten the slings, then slowly raise the machine just off the ground and pause. Check the lifting eyes, slings, center of gravity, and machine position. Proceed only after confirming that everything is stable and secure.

   <div style="position:absolute;top:-14px;left:10px;background:#fff;border:1px solid #d93025;border-radius:2px;padding:2px 10px;color:#d93025;font-weight:700;line-height:22px;">⚡ Danger</div>

4. Slowly and steadily raise the machine to the required height, keeping it level.

   <div class="c500-callout c500-callout-danger" style="position:relative;border:1px solid #d93025;border-left:6px solid #d93025;border-radius:3px;margin:25px 0 16px;padding:1px 18px 12px;background:#fff;">

   <div style="padding-top:0px;line-height:1.75;color:#333;"><br>- Avoid sudden lifting, abrupt stops, or excessive swinging.<br>- Do not stand beneath the suspended load.</div>

5. Move the machine horizontally to the target position above the pallet, then slowly lower it until the pallet rests stably on the ground. Once stable, release and remove the slings, and inspect the outer wooden crate and pallet. 

#### Handling the Machine with a Forklift or Hydraulic Lift Truck

**Precautions**

- Use a forklift or hydraulic lift truck with a rated load of at least 250 kg.
- Before handling the machine, confirm that the load requirements, route, and placement area are suitable for both the machine and the handling equipment.
- Support the machine from beneath its base and take the necessary measures to prevent slipping and tipping.

**Procedure**

1. Inspect the wooden crate and bottom pallet to ensure they are intact and securely fastened. Confirm that the total weight of the crate, the forklift's rated capacity, and the handling route meet the requirements.

2. Position the forklift at the designated fork-entry side of the pallet. Adjust the fork spacing and align the forks with the pallet's load-bearing areas. Slowly insert the forks, ensuring they are fully inserted into the pallet without contacting the crate or machine.

<p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/handing4.jpg" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
   </p>

3. Raise the crate slightly and check that the pallet is stable and the crate is not tilted or slipping. Once confirmed, raise the crate to the minimum height required for transport.

4. Move slowly to the destination. Avoid sharp turns, sudden braking, and uneven surfaces. Keep the crate low while moving, and watch for nearby people and obstacles.

<p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/handing5.jpg" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
   </p>

5. At the destination, slowly lower the crate until the pallet rests securely on the ground. Once the crate is stable, fully lower the forks and carefully withdraw them.
