### Handling Safety Requirements

#### Operating Requirements

- The machine is heavy. It must not be moved manually by one or two people. At least four people must work together to lift, carry, push, pull, or drag the machine.
- Handling personnel must wear personal protective equipment (PPE), including but not limited to protective footwear, protective gloves, and safety helmets.
- Before handling, make sure the route is clear of obstacles and the floor is level and solid.
- When handling the machine by hoisting, do not raise it rapidly, stop abruptly, or pull it at an angle. Doing so may cause the machine to tilt, swing, or tip over.
- When using a forklift or hydraulic lift truck, support the machine from beneath its base and take the necessary measures to prevent slipping and tipping.

#### Handling Equipment Requirements

- Use a forklift, hydraulic lift truck, gantry crane, jib crane, or other suitable handling equipment to move the machine. The equipment's rated load must be no less than the total weight of the machine. A hydraulic lift truck, gantry crane, or jib crane must have a rated load of at least 250 kg.
- The lifting mechanism must have a reliable braking system. Do not use lifting equipment that is defective or has not been inspected.
- When hoisting the machine, do not use ropes, wire, belts, or makeshift slings without a rated-load label. Use suitable lifting eyes, slings, and other rigging equipment for lifting. Ensure that their rated load capacities meet the requirements specified in this section.
