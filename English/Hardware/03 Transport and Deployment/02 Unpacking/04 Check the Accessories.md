### Check the Accessories

1. After moving the machine to its final operating location, slowly open the acrylic protective door. Check the door for cracks, deformation, looseness, or other abnormalities, and confirm that it opens and closes smoothly.

   <div class="c500-callout c500-callout-danger" style="position:relative;border:1px solid #d93025;border-left:6px solid #d93025;border-radius:3px;margin:25px 0 16px;padding:1px 18px 12px;background:#fff;">
   <div style="position:absolute;top:-14px;left:10px;background:#fff;border:1px solid #d93025;border-radius:2px;padding:2px 10px;color:#d93025;font-weight:700;line-height:22px;">⚡ Danger</div>
   <div style="padding-top:0px;line-height:1.75;color:#333;"><br>- At least four people must work together to lift, carry, push, pull, or drag the machine.</div>
   </div>


<p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/unpacking7.png" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">

2. Check the machine surface for scratches, dents, paint damage, deformation, or other visible damage. Confirm that the main unit exterior and the initial internal protective condition are intact.

3. After completing the machine appearance inspection, remove the accessories and packaging boxes from inside the machine in order from top to bottom and from outside to inside.


4. Remove the EPE foam block at the front of the machine and the front accessory packaging box.

<p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/unpacking8.png" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
   </p>

5. Remove the tool kit, standard accessories, maintenance consumables, and other supplied accessories.

<p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/unpacking9.png" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
   </p>


   <p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/unpacking27.png" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
   </p>

6. Remove the raw material packaging boxes and related accessories inside the machine and on the worktable.

<p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/unpacking28.png" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
   </p>

7. Arrange all accessories together and check whether the packaging is damaged, damp, deformed, or abnormal.

8. Check the type and quantity of each accessory and confirm that there are no missing, damaged, or mismatched items.
