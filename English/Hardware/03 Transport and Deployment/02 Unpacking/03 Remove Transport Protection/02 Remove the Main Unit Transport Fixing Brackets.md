#### Remove the Main Unit Transport Fixing Brackets

After removing the external packaging, remove the four fixing brackets between the main unit and the transport pallet as follows.

1. Use a 5 mm hex key to remove the fixing screws from the four groups of main unit transport fixing brackets, then remove all transport fixing brackets in order.

   <p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/unpacking5.png" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
   </p>

2. Remove the brackets smoothly. Do not pry or strike the machine body by force to prevent scratches, structural deformation, or screw damage.

3. Slowly remove the inner packaging bag, taking care not to scratch the machine surface.

   <p align="center">
       <img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/c500-manual/unpacking6.png" alt="" width="400px" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
   </p>

4. With the main unit transport handles, spindle and worktable fixing bracket, and internal transport protective materials left in place, follow the [Handling Safety Requirements](https://docs.worksbase.com/en/transport-deployment#handling-safety-requirements) to move the machine to its final operating location and place it securely on the worktable.
