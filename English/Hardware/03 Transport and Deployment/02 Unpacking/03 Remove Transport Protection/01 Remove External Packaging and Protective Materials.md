#### Remove External Packaging and Protective Materials

After completing the outer packaging inspection and confirming that the packaging is intact, remove the transport protection materials as follows. During removal, avoid direct contact between sharp tools and the machine body, protective door, camera, probe, and other components to prevent scratches or damage.

1. Remove the external wooden crate. Prepare a flat-head screwdriver and pliers. Insert the screwdriver into the opening of the metal clip and pry it open, then grip and straighten the clip with the pliers.

![](assets/01-f659d8cbf342.png){width=10cm}

2. Check the inner packaging protection of the machine. The machine body is wrapped with a dedicated dustproof bag. The sides and corners of the machine are protected by dedicated EPE foam to absorb vibration and impact during transportation.



3. Check whether the dustproof bag is intact, without damage or cracks. Check whether the corner EPE foam fits the machine properly, without shifting, breaking, or falling off. Confirm that the overall protective structure is intact, without transportation displacement or protection failure.



4. After confirming that the inner packaging protection is normal, gently remove the dustproof bag outside the machine and remove the external EPE foam and other protective materials.
