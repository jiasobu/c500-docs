### Machine Support Table Requirements

The machine must be placed on a support table or installation platform that meets the following requirements, before installation, leveling, and machining operations.

**Dimensions**

At least 800 mm × 800 mm of support space is recommended to allow sufficient room for operation, maintenance, and cable routing.

**Load Capacity**

The table must have a rated load capacity of at least 250 kg.

The machine is equipped with an 800 W high\-speed spindle (maximum speed: 18,000 r/min) and stepper motors. Machining may generate significant vibration, emergency\-stop forces, and dynamic impact loads. The table's maximum load capacity must be at least twice the machine's net weight, with the maximum workpiece and fixture weights added. The safety factor must be at least 2\.0.

The table surface must support at least 400 kg/m² of uniformly distributed load.

The machine is supported by four feet, each measuring 71 mm × 71 mm. Because the center of gravity is toward the rear, the localized pressure under each foot may reach approximately 1\.43 kg/cm² (approximately 140 kPa), which is considerably higher than the uniformly distributed load requirement. The table surface material must withstand this localized pressure. If it cannot, place a steel plate or high\-strength load-distribution plate at least 5 mm thick beneath the machine to distribute the load.

**Stability and Rigidity**

The table must have adequate structural rigidity, vibration resistance, and dynamic stability. It must not wobble, tilt, or show visible deformation.

A suitable installation platform should provide sufficient strength and rigidity. Heavy cast\-iron or welded steel\-frame tables are recommended\. If the tabletop is made of a soft material (e.g., ordinary wood), place a steel plate or high-strength load-distribution plate at least 5 mm thick beneath the machine to distribute the load. A support platform with insufficient stability or structural strength may amplify machining vibration and affect machining accuracy.

For a mobile table with casters, the casters must have reliable locking mechanisms and must remain locked during machining operation.

**Leveling Capability**

The table must allow the machine to be leveled, and the platform surface must be flat.

The surface flatness deviation must be no more than 1 mm/m. Before installing the machine, check the table surface with a spirit level to ensure that the base surface is generally level. After installation, the machine must be precisely leveled using its leveling feet.

For easy leveling, use a table with adjustable feet or another installation platform with leveling functions.

**Vibration Damping and Noise Reduction**

When the machine is installed on a floor or in another environment where vibration may be transmitted, it is recommended to take appropriate vibration damping measures based on site conditions to reduce the impact of vibration and noise on machining operations and the surrounding environment.

For high\-precision machining scenarios, the rigidity and vibration-dampening performance of the installation platform can be further enhanced according to actual requirements.
