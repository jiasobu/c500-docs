### Power Supply and Safety Instructions


Use the power cord supplied with the machine that complies with applicable local standards. Connect it to a compatible power outlet with a properly functioning protective ground connection.

The recommended circuit breakers for the customer-side power supply circuit are listed below:

| **Input Voltage** | **Maximum Input Current** | **Recommended Circuit Breaker** |
| :---------------: | :-----------------------: | :-----------------------------: |
| 110 V AC | 15 A | 2P, C20 or D20 |
| 220 V AC | 10 A | 2P, C16 or D16 |

The selection and installation of circuit breakers and residual current devices (RCDs) must comply with applicable local electrical regulations. When multiple machines operate simultaneously, the cumulative leakage current may cause the RCDs to trip.
