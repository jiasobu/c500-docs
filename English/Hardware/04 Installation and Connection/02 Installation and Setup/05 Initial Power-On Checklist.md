### Initial Power-On Checklist


| **No.** | **Items** | **Requirements** |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 01 | Installation Environment | The machine is placed in a dry, clean, and well-lit indoor area, free from flammable liquids, gases, and combustible dust. |
| 02 | Machine Placement | The machine is positioned securely on a flat, level surface with sufficient load-bearing capacity to support the machine. |
| 03 | Machine Condition | No visible shipping damage, deformation, or loose components. |
| 04 | Protective Door | No cracks, deformation, or looseness. Opens and closes smoothly. |
| 05 | External Shipping Brackets | All four sets of shipping brackets and their corresponding mounting screws have been removed. |
| 06 | Spindle and Worktable Bracket | The shipping bracket between the spindle and worktable has been removed. |
| 07 | Spindle-Side Screws | The supplied spindle-side sheet metal mounting screws have been reinstalled in their designated holes. |
| 08 | Packaging and Accessories | All packaging boxes, loose accessories, and tools have been removed from the enclosure and worktable. |
| 09 | Remaining Shipping Materials | All foam inserts, spacers, tape, and other shipping materials that can be safely removed while the machine is powered off have been removed, except for the support block beneath the spindle. See the note below the table. |
| 10 | Air Nozzle Caps | The protective caps have been removed from the internal air nozzles and the spindle air nozzle. |
| 11 | Tool-Clamping Protective Cover | The protective cover at the spindle tool-clamping area has been removed. |
| 12 | Component Protective Films and Stickers | Protective films or stickers have been removed from the camera, lighting, probe, protective door, and other components. |
| 13 | Tool Magazine Protection | Shipping tape and protective film have been removed from the tool magazine. |
| 14 | Input Power | The supply voltage and frequency match the ratings specified on the machine nameplate. |
| 15 | Protective Grounding | The power outlet is properly grounded, and the grounding terminal is intact. |
| 16 | Power Cord and Plug | No visible damage, deformation, or other abnormalities. |
| 17 | Cable Routing | Visible cables are free from pinching or excessive tension and do not interfere with machine movement. |
| 18 | Accessory Connections | Accessories are securely connected according to the installation instructions while the machine is powered off. Do not connect or disconnect accessories while the machine is powered on. |
| 19 | Tool Magazine Mounting | The tool magazine is correctly positioned, and all mounting screws are tightened. |
| 20 | Tool Magazine Connections | The tool magazine connectors are connected and secured according to the installation instructions. |
| 21 | Air Hose | No visible damage or pinching. All connections are secure. |
| 22 | Water Tank Positioning | The water tank is stable, the fill cap is securely tightened, and the vent valve faces upward. |
| 23 | Coolant Level | The coolant level is within the marked range, and the copper filter element is submerged below the coolant surface. |
| 24 | Water Lines | No damage or kinks. All connections are secure. |
| 25 | Work Area | No loose tools, accessories, or other obstructions remain inside the enclosure or machine travel area. |
| 26 | E-Stop Button | No visible damage. The button is clearly identifiable and easily accessible. |
