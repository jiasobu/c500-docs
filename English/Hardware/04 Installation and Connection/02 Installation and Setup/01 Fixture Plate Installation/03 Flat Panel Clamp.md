#### Flat Panel Clamp

**Preparation**  
  
Before installation, prepare the following materials and tools: flat panel clamps ×4, M6×25 / M6×35 T-slot bolts ×8, M6 flange nuts ×8, custom auxiliary blocks ×2, 5 mm hex key, and 10 mm Allen wrench.

**Installation Steps**  

1. Place the flat panel clamps and auxiliary blocks as shown in the illustration.  
2. Tighten or loosen the clamp’s pressure nut to secure or release the workpiece.  Suitable for large workpiece blanks that are subject to relatively high machining loads.

![](assets/Flat_Panel_Clamp-f04edf6067d0.png){width=12cm}


| No. | Name                   |
| --- | ---------------------- |
| 1   | Flat Plate Clamp       |
| 2   | Workpiece Blank        |
| 3   | Auxiliary Spacer Block |



> [!caution] Caution
> - Tighten the mounting screws with appropriate force. Do not over-tighten them, as this may damage the threads on the worktable or clamp plates.  
> - Ensure the clamp plates remain parallel after installation to prevent uneven clamping force or machining deviation.  
> - Avoid direct contact between the clamp plates and the workpiece. Use a soft cloth or protective pad to prevent surface scratches.
