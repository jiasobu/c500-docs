#### L-Shaped Locating Fixture + OK Clamp

**Preparation**  
  
Before installation, prepare the following materials and tools: L-shaped locating fixture ×1, OK clamp ×2, φ4×10 locating pins ×2, M6×12 / M6×20 hex socket screws ×3, M6×20 T-slot bolts ×4, M6 flange nuts ×4, 5 mm hex key, and 10 mm Allen wrench.

> [!note]  
> The T-slot bolt is used to tighten and secure the fixture set. To use it, insert the T-shaped head into the slot on the worktable in the correct orientation, rotate it 90°, and then tighten the nut.

**Installation Steps**  

1. Place the L-shaped locating fixture and OK clamp as shown in the illustration.  
2. Tighten or loosen the OK clamp’s top screw to secure or release the workpiece. Suitable for workpiece blanks with consistent dimensions, where quick loading and unloading are required and machining loads are relatively low.
