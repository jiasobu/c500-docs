### Pressure Adjustment

The output pressure can be adjusted using the pressure adjustment knob on the air compressor. Ensure the output pressure always remains within the required operating range. Excessive pressure may damage the machine air system, while insufficient pressure may affect chip blowing and cooling performance.  


> [!caution] Caution
> - The air compressor generates noise and heat during operation. Do not approach the air outlet or touch high-temperature surfaces to avoid burns.  
> - Regularly check the air hose and interface connections during use to prevent air leaks caused by loose connections.  
> - If abnormal conditions such as prolonged continuous operation, abnormal pressure readings, or unusual noise occur, immediately disconnect the power and contact qualified service personnel for inspection and repair.




> [!note] Note
> Perform air compressor maintenance according to the following intervals.
> 1. Drain the air tank at least once a week.
> 2. Inspect the safety valve once a month.
> 3. Clean the air filter element every 200 operating hours and replace it every 500 operating hours.
> 4. Inspect the internal and external surfaces of the air tank once a year, and perform a pressure test every two years.
