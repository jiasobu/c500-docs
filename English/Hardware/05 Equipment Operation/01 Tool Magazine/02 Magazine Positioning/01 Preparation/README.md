#### Preparation

Prepare a 3mm hex key for the operation.  

Move the spindle to the leftmost limit and highest point, and position the table toward the operator side; power off and lock out the machine to ensure operational safety.

> [!caution] Caution
> Adhesive remover may be used to remove AB adhesive residue from the worktable. Do not spray or apply adhesive remover to the tool magazine cover, as it may cause corrosion.
