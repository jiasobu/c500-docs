### Test Methodology and Notes

**Machining Range**

Verified by testing the full travel of all axes and machining representative workpieces from the machining checklist.

Axis travel refers to the movement range of each linear axis, not the maximum workpiece dimensions that can be machined. Actual machining dimensions may vary depending on tool length, fixturing, workpiece geometry, and setup position.

**Positioning Accuracy and Positioning Repeatability**

The X, Y, and Z linear axes were measured using a laser interferometer. Positioning accuracy was evaluated based on the deviation between the commanded and measured positions at each target location. Repeatability is evaluated based on the variation in measured positions over multiple approaches to the same target point.

Positioning accuracy and repeatability are indicators of machine motion performance and do not represent the dimensional tolerances of finished workpieces. Actual machining results may be affected by factors such as material, tooling, workholding, cutting parameters, and environmental conditions.

**Operating Noise**

Noise is measured using an A-weighted sound level meter at a distance of 1 m from the machine during aluminum alloy cutting in an anechoic test room. The rated operating noise level is ≤ 70 dB(A).

Testing is conducted indoors under controlled background noise conditions. Actual measurement results may vary depending on the material, tooling, cutting parameters, machine installation, and environmental conditions.

**Continuous Operating Time**

The machine is powered on and operated continuously during the test. The continuous operating time is no less than 8 hours.

This parameter indicates the duration of continuous machine operation. Actual machining performance may vary depending on the operating mode, load, and machining conditions.
