## Warranty

**1. General**


This policy applies to new NestWorks C500 machines and official accessories purchased from authorized sales channels, including official stores and authorized resellers.  
The warranty period begins on the day following delivery. Valid proof of purchase is required.  
This warranty is limited to defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions.  
This warranty policy supplements, and does not affect or replace, any mandatory statutory rights available to users under applicable law.


**2. Return Policy**

- **7-Day Quality Return**: If a manufacturing defect is verified within 7 days of delivery, NestWorks will provide a full refund and cover all associated return shipping costs.
- **Non-Returnable Conditions**: Returns will not be accepted for products that have been used, modified, or damaged, or for items with missing components, expired return windows, or lack of proof of purchase.

**3. Replacement Policy**

- **15-Day Replacement**: NestWorks provides free replacement for verified manufacturing defects or shipping damage, within 15 days of delivery.
- **Warranty Restart**: Upon delivery of a replacement unit, the warranty period will restart from the new date of delivery.

**Coverage and Limitations**


**Warranty Covers:** 

- This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under proper use and maintenance.

**Warranty Does NOT Cover:**

1. Unauthorized disassembly of the machine spindle, control system, laser module, or safety interlock structure; tampering with equipment firmware or system parameters.
    
2. Use of cutting tools, coolants, or processing materials explicitly prohibited in the operation manual, resulting in corrosion, spindle burnout, or laser lens damage.
    
3. Collision or structural deformation caused by failure to remove factory transport fasteners during transportation and installation.
    
4. Long-term operation beyond the rated limits of temperature, humidity, altitude, or load.
    
5. Intrusion of dust, standing water, or corrosive gas into the equipment interior.
    
6. Hardware burnout caused by man-made collision, dropping, liquid submersion, fire, lightning strike, or unstable power supply voltage.
    
7. Normal wear and tear of consumable parts generated during regular operation, including cutting tools, filter elements, brushes, and seals. Consumables are not covered under free warranty.
    
8. Second-hand resale, purchase through unofficial channels, or altered or missing serial number (SN) on the machine body.
    
9. Application in scenarios beyond the designed intended use, such as mass continuous industrial production, machining confidential military components, or machining medical implants or components related to life-support systems.


**5. Service Process**

- **Contact Support**: If you need warranty, repair, return or replacement, or spare parts support, please contact service@nestworks.ai and provide the device SN, order number, and relevant photos or videos of the issue.
- **Remote Diagnosis**: Support will provide remote diagnosis, and troubleshooting guidance.
- **Repair Service**: NestWorks will arrange spare parts shipment, or machine repair services.
- **Shipping**: NestWorks covers fees for warranty cases, while the customer is responsible for non-warranty costs.

**6. Overseas and Cross-Border Service**


NestWorks does not provide a global warranty, and services are valid only within the original region of purchase.
For cross-border support, the customer is responsible for shipping and taxes to the service center, while NestWorks covers the return shipping costs.


**7. Liability**

NestWorks’ liability is limited to the repair, replacement, or refund of the product. NestWorks is not responsible for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.


**8. Final Provisions**


NestWorks reserves the right to update this warranty policy. In principle, the version of the warranty policy in effect at the time of product purchase shall apply, and subsequent updates shall not apply retroactively to products already sold.
