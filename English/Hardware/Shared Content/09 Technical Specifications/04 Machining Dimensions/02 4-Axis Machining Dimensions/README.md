### 4-Axis Machining Dimensions


1. Tool magazine placed along the Y-axis, with tailstock center

When clamping Φ80 mm stock, the maximum machinable diameter is Φ100 mm, and the machinable length along the X-axis is 175 mm.

2. Tool magazine on the X-axis right side, with tailstock center 

When the tool magazine is placed on the X-axis right side and the tailstock center is used, the machinable length along the X-axis is 70 mm.

![](../../../../../../assets/Machinable_Area_2-fd7b7992386d.png){width=10cm}

<!-- mdwb:figure id=49c7e5f5ab8d5102 -->
Figure 9-5 4-Axis Machining Dimensions
<!-- /mdwb:figure -->


3. Tool magazine on the X-axis right side, without tailstock center 

When the tool magazine is placed on the X-axis right side and the tailstock center is not used, the machinable length varies depending on the stock diameter:
- For stock diameters of Φ60–Φ100 mm, the machinable length along the X-axis is 103 mm.
- For stock diameters less than Φ60 mm, the machinable length along the X-axis is 130 mm.
