#### Remove the Main Unit Transport Fixing Brackets

After removing the external packaging, remove the four fixing brackets between the main unit and the transport pallet as follows.

1. Use a 5 mm hex key to remove the fixing screws from the four groups of main unit transport fixing brackets, then remove all transport fixing brackets in order.

![](../../../../../assets/Transport_Bracket_Removal_01-d10a26fc7d7b.png){width=12cm}

2. Remove the brackets smoothly. Do not pry or strike the machine body by force to prevent scratches, structural deformation, or screw damage.

![](../../../../../assets/Transport_Bracket_Removal_02-e87be29f004e.png){width=12cm}

3. Slowly remove the inner packaging bag, taking care not to scratch the machine surface.

![](../../../../../assets/04_2-c4e8e5dd3949.png){width=10cm}

4. Remove the securing tape from the surface of the machine.

![](../../../../../assets/05_1-d94c83033c4d.png){width=10cm}

5. With the main unit transport handles, spindle and worktable fixing bracket, and internal transport protective materials left in place, follow the [Handling Safety Requirements](https://docs.worksbase.com/en/transport-deployment#handling-safety-requirements) to move the machine to its final operating location and place it securely on the worktable.
