### Dust Collection Kit Installation

The dust collection kit is used to collect chips and dust generated during machining, helping maintain a clean working area and reducing the impact of dust on the machine and operating environment.

**Installation Preparation**

Before installation, confirm that all dust collection kit components are included: dust collector unit, dust hose, dust port connector, and mounting clips.

**Installation Steps**

1. Align the slot on the dust port with the mounting slot on the spindle and insert the dust port. A noticeable locking sensation during insertion indicates that it is properly installed.

![](../../../../assets/Installation_Steps_01-d6a070150235.jpg){width=12cm}

2.  Install the brush bracket at the upper-left corner of the worktable, then tighten the thumb screw.

> [!caution] Caution
> With the dust collection brush installed, only workpieces with a maximum surface height difference of 20 mm or less can be machined. If the height difference exceeds this range, remove the dust collection brush before machining.

3. Insert the L-shaped adapter into the round opening at the upper-right side of the machine enclosure, then secure it with the locking nut.

![](../../../../assets/Installation_Steps_03-614c58d6be18.jpg){width=12cm}

4. Connect the threaded end of the dust hose to the L-shaped adapter and tighten it securely. 

![](../../../../assets/Installation_Steps_04-82c6d2f44526.jpg){width=12cm}

5. Insert the other end of the dust hose into the rear connector of the dust collection brush to complete the installation.

![](../../../../assets/Installation_Steps_05-668bc3c414ca.jpg){width=12cm}

**Usage and Cleaning**

- During machining, it is recommended to keep the dust collector enabled to remove chips and dust promptly, preventing dust accumulation that may affect machining accuracy and machine lifespan.  

- After machining is completed, turn off the dust collector and disconnect the power supply. Clean chips and dust from the dust collection box, and clean the dust hose and dust port to prevent blockage.  

- Regularly inspect the dust collector filter. If the filter becomes clogged, clean or replace it promptly to maintain proper suction performance.
