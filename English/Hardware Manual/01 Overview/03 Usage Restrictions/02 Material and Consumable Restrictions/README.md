### Material and Consumable Restrictions

- It is prohibited to machine hazardous materials such as asbestos, cyanide-containing substances, and highly toxic volatile substances. When machining carbon fiber, epoxy resin, or similar materials, the supporting dust collection system must be activated and professional respiratory protection must be worn.
- It is prohibited to machine high-hardness and brittle materials such as ceramics and cemented carbide, or materials with risks of flammability, explosion, or spontaneous combustion, such as magnesium powder and graphite.
- It is prohibited to use cutting tools that exceed the spindle specifications or the tool magazine compatibility range. Do not use damaged, deformed, or non-standard tools for machining.
- The Company shall not be liable for personal poisoning, fires, environmental pollution, or environmental penalties arising from the unauthorized machining of toxic, flammable, or harmful materials.
