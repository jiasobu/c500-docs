## Unpacking



The machine unpacking inspection is used to verify that the machine and its accessories have not been damaged or lost during transportation. Handle all packaging materials carefully during unpacking and do not use force to remove them.

> [!danger] Danger  
> Due to the heavy weight of the machine, pay attention to personal safety during unpacking. It is recommended to take photos or videos of the packaging condition before unpacking. This helps with later inspection of transportation damage or after-sales service.
