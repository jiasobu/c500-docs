#### Magazine Removal

a. Loosen the lock nut on the right-side pneumatic connector of the tool magazine and carefully remove the connector.  

![](../../../../../assets/Magazine_Removal_01_0-2d4d4112826f.jpg){width=12cm}

![](../../../../../assets/Magazine_Removal_01_1-ee5f7641658d.jpg){width=12cm}

b. Flip open the magazine cover and remove the four M4×60 screws securing the magazine.

c. Hold the magazine with both hands and lift it vertically; retain the two 4×10 dowel pins and ensure they are not lost.

![](../../../../../assets/Magazine_Removal_02-d635b018453d.jpg){width=12cm}
