### Location Requirements

- The machine net weight is 130 kg and shall be placed on a flat, level, and stable worktable with sufficient load-bearing capacity. The rated load capacity of the worktable shall be no less than 250 kg, and the floor load-bearing capacity of the installation area shall be no less than 250 kg/m². Do not stack two or more machines directly on top of each other for installation or storage.
- The workbench must be stable, without wobbling, tilting, or visible deformation.
- After installing the machine, check its placement and adjust the leveling pads if necessary to ensure that the machine remains level.
- Keep the work area clean, dry, well lit, and properly ventilated. Dust, smoke, oil mist, or other volatile substances generated during machining should be removed promptly. Use ventilation, dust collection, or air purification measures as needed to prevent indoor accumulation.
- The machine must be shielded from direct sunlight, rain, condensation, heat sources, and other environmental factors that could affect its normal operation.
- Do not place combustible materials such as cardboard, wood chips, cloth, plastics, or other debris around the machine.
- During operation, keep children, pets, and other unauthorized personnel away from the machining area and the machine.
- Ensure sufficient space around the machine for operation and maintenance.



> [!caution] Caution  
> To achieve more stable machining accuracy and machine performance, it is recommended to use the equipment under constant temperature conditions at approximately 25 °C.
