### Machine Safety

- The machine is heavy. Take appropriate safety measures during handling, transport, and installation.
- Do not machine beyond the travel limits to prevent collisions, flying debris, or machine damage.
- Workpieces must be securely clamped with standard fixtures. Never support workpieces by hand during machining.
- Start automatic machining only when the protective door is fully closed and the safety interlock is functioning properly.
- Do not place any part of the body inside the machining area while the machine is running.
- Do not remove, short-circuit, or bypass any safety protection device.
- Do not touch moving parts, the spindle, or rotating tools during operation.
