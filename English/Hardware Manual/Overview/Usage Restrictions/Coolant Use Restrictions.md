### Coolant Use Restrictions


- Purified water is recommended as the machine coolant. After use, promptly remove any residual liquid from inside the machine, wipe the relevant metal surfaces dry, and apply rust prevention treatment.

- When using plant-oil-based, mineral-oil-based, fully synthetic, or semi-synthetic water-based coolants that meet machine requirements, select the coolant type according to the material being machined, and strictly follow the product instructions, recommended mixing ratio, and usage requirements.

- Different types of cutting fluids may pose varying risks: plant-oil-based and mineral-oil-based coolants may pose fire, oil mist, and skin contact hazards; fully synthetic coolants may cause skin and eye irritation; semi-synthetic water-based coolants may lead to microbial growth, odor, and corrosion risks. Users must strictly follow the product instructions and safety requirements of the cutting fluid purchased, and take appropriate protective measures.
- When using the above coolants, pay attention to fire safety, occupational health, and workplace environmental safety risks. Do not use any coolant under conditions exceeding its flash point.
- Failure to use coolant in compliance with specifications resulting in spindle corrosion or fire shall void all warranty coverage.
