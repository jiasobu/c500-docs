## Configuring Machining Parameters

Before starting machining, configure the material, model, and machining parameters as required:

1. Click **Model** in the process tree on the left or on the workbench to open the **Model** Settings panel. Adjust the model's dimensions, position, rotation, and alignment as needed.
![](https://docs.worksbase.com/neststudio/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87/model-settings-en.png)<!-- publication-image: {width=12cm} -->

2. Click **Material Size** in the process tree on the left to open the Stock Settings panel and configure the origin position, material dimensions, and material type.
![](https://docs.worksbase.com/neststudio/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87/material-size-en.png)<!-- publication-image: {width=12cm} -->

3. Click **Fixture** in the process tree on the left, then select the fixture type from the drop-down menu on the right. The workbench will automatically render the corresponding fixture model and adjust the fixture's clamping opening to fit the current stock dimensions.
![](https://docs.worksbase.com/neststudio/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87/fixture-settings-en.png)<!-- publication-image: {width=12cm} -->

4. Click **Create Toolpath**, the system automatically generates a "One-Click SmartPath" process card. No manual tool or machining parameter configuration is required for the initial machining operation.
![](https://docs.worksbase.com/neststudio/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87/oneclick-smartpath-en.png)<!-- publication-image: {width=12cm} -->
