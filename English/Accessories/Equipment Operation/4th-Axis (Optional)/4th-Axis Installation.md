### 4th-Axis Installation

The 4th-Axis is an optional component, mainly used for engraving cylindrical, conical, and other rotary workpieces, enabling multi-face and multi-angle engraving, and improving machining flexibility.

Typical machining applications include cylindrical engraving, circular pattern machining, and multi-face rotary engraving, such as pen holders, cylindrical ornaments, and conical surface engraving.
