### Workpiece Clamping


The electric vise is equipped with **Clamp** and **Release** control buttons.

1. Press the **Release** button on the vise, adjust the opening width to a suitable position, then place the workpiece blank into the vise.

![](../../../../../assets/Workpiece_Clamping_01-f27fe0df4645.jpeg){width=12cm}

2. Place the workpiece blank into the vise clamping area, then press and hold the **Clamp** button to perform the initial clamping.

![](../../../../../assets/Workpiece_Clamping_02-6c04c5cc8319.jpeg){width=12cm}

3. After the vise clamps the workpiece, and the control screen displays the message “Please tighten the workpiece with the wrench,” use the supplied wrench to fully tighten the vise.

![](../../../../../assets/Workpiece_Clamping_03-69b400956b48.jpeg){width=12cm}

4. After machining is completed, loosen the vise with the wrench first, then press and hold the **Release** button to remove the workpiece.

![](../../../../../assets/Workpiece_Clamping_04-4c1558aaf2bc.jpeg){width=12cm}
