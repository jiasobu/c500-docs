#### Clamping a Cylindrical Workpiece for Relief Machining


When clamping a cylindrical workpiece for relief machining, make sure the relief machining area extends completely outside the vise. This prevents the vise jaws from blocking the tool and causing a tool collision.

> [!caution] Caution  
> The stock must be clamped before machining to prevent the workpiece from loosening or shifting during cutting, which may scrap the workpiece or damage the tool.

> [!note] Note  
> After daily machining is finished, it is recommended to release the vise to relieve stress on the vise jaws and prevent long-term clamping from deforming mechanical components.
