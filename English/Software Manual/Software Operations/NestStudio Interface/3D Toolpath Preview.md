### 3D Toolpath Preview

In the Preview interface, users can review the dynamic toolpath trajectories, estimated machining time, and toolpath distribution. Click **Export** to export the G-code file.

The Preview interface consists of two main sections: G-code Preview on the left and the 3D Toolpath Simulation Viewport on the right. By default, the interface displays the simulation effects of all toolpaths combined; to inspect an individual toolpath, check only the target object.
