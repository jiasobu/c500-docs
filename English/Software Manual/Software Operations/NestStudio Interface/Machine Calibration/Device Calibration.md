#### Device Calibration

Allows precision calibration and parameter adjustment to correct positioning deviations and improve machining safety and accuracy.

**Device Diagnostics**

- Safety Mode 
  Enables device safety protection. Keeping this function enabled during machining is recommended.

- Machining Assistance 
  Controls functions such as air purification, vacuum suction, spindle cleaning, coolant, and worktable cleaning.  
  
  When enabled, it helps improve chip removal and cooling performance.


- Lighting and Vision

  Controls lighting and framing laser functions for workpiece inspection and positioning verification.

- Mechanism and Tools

  Controls tool magazine opening and closing for tool replacement and maintenance.

**Device Calibration**

Users can manually adjust the upward and downward positions of the touch probe and correct probe positioning errors to improve centering accuracy.


> [!caution] Caution
> Do not touch the workpiece or probe during centering and calibration.
