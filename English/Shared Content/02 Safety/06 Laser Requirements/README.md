## Laser Requirements


The laser operates in an enclosed safety cover. The protective door must be closed and locked before processing. If the cover is not enclosed, the operator must wear laser safety goggles for the specific wavelength.

Please follow these safety rules:
- Do not operate the machine if any protective parts are missing.
- It is prohibited to cut or engrave PVC, vinyl, or chlorine-containing plastics. These materials release toxic and corrosive gases that damage the machine and void the warranty.
- Avoid processing materials with unknown ingredients to prevent harmful fumes.
- Do not leave the machine unattended; you must stay with the machine during the entire process.
- You must turn on the exhaust or air purification system to ensure ventilation.
- The chip outlet cover must be closed during laser processing.
