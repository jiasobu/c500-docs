### Semi-Automatic Centering

1. On the home page, tap **Origin** to enter the Workpiece Origin Settings page.

2. Select **Manual**, then tap **Confirm**.

3. Use the spindle movement panel on the right to move the spindle above the workpiece, then tap **Start**. The probe will extend automatically.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/半自动分中1-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

4. Once the probe extends, move the spindle to align it with the workpiece center. Tap **Set Z**. The probe will then descend slowly to contact the workpiece surface and record the Z value.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/半自动分中2-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

5. Move the probe to the left side of the workpiece and tap **Set X1**. The probe then moves slowly to the right to touch the workpiece's left edge and record the X1 value.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/半自动分中3-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

6. Move the probe to the right side of the workpiece and tap **Set X2**. The probe then moves slowly to the left to touch the workpiece's right edge and record the X2 value.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/半自动分中4-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

7. Move the probe to the rear of the workpiece and tap **Set Y1_a**. The probe then moves slowly forward to touch the workpiece's rear edge and record the first Y1 measurement.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/半自动分中5-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

8. Move the probe a short distance along the workpiece's rear edge, then tap **Set Y1_b**. The probe then moves slowly forward to touch the rear edge and record the second Y1 measurement.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/半自动分中6-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

   <div class="c500-callout c500-callout-note" style="position:relative;border:1px solid #2e9d62;border-left:6px solid #2e9d62;border-radius:3px;margin:22px 0 16px;padding:16px 18px 12px;background:#fff;">
   <div style="position:absolute;top:-14px;left:10px;background:#fff;border:1px solid #2e9d62;border-radius:2px;padding:2px 10px;color:#2e9d62;font-weight:700;line-height:22px;">ⓘ Note</div>
   <div style="padding-top:2px;line-height:1.75;color:#333;">Two points are measured along the workpiece's rear edge to calculate its tilt angle and compensate for misalignment with the machine axes.</div></div>
   

9. Move the probe to the front of the workpiece and tap **Set Y2**. The probe then moves slowly backward to touch the workpiece's front edge and record the Y2 value.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/半自动分中7-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

10. The probe retracts automatically, and the spindle moves to the calculated workpiece origin. When "Centering Completed" appears, the X, Y, and Z-axis origin coordinates are displayed.
