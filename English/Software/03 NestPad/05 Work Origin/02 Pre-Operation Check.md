### Pre-Operation Check

- Before starting centering, please confirm that the equipment has completed mechanical homing.
- Confirm that the probe is correctly installed and that the probe calibration value is still valid.
- Securely clamp the workpiece. Clean the workpiece surface and work surface, ensuring they are free of chips and debris.
