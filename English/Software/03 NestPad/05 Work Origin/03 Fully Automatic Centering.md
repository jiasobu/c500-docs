### Fully Automatic Centering

1. On the home page, tap **Origin** to enter the Workpiece Origin Settings page.

2. Select **Probe**, then tap **Confirm**.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/分中模式-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

3. Use the spindle movement panel on the right to move the spindle above the workpiece. Make sure the workpiece is centered in the camera view, then tap **Next**.


<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/全自动分中1-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

4. Select the recognition method according to the actual situation. After the workpiece dimensions are detected, tap **Start**.

<p align="center"><img src="https://docs.worksbase.com/nestpad/英文/全自动分中2-en.png" alt="" width="480" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;"></p>

| **Recognition Method**   | **Description**                                  |
| :--------                | :------------------------------------------------|
| Scan QR Code             | Scan the QR code on the workpiece to automatically retrieve its length, width, and height.   |
| Scan Work piece          | Automatically recognize the workpiece contour and dimensions.                            |
| Manual Select            | Manually define the workpiece dimensions.                            |

5. Follow the on-screen instructions to perform automatic centering. When the message "Centering Completed" appears, the origin coordinates for the X, Y, and Z axes are displayed.
