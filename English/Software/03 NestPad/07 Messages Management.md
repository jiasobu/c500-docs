## Messages Manaagement

The Message page consolidates device alarms, faults, and notification messages to help users quickly identify issues and resolve them by following the suggested solutions.

On the home page, tap **Messages** to open the message list. Select a message to view fault details, then follow the on-screen instructions to troubleshoot the issue and clear the device alarm.
