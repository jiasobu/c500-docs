### User Guide


Users can access the **User Guide** through the left menu bar on the Home page to master the core operations of NestStudio, obtain professional tool selection advice, and learn CNC machining knowledge to ensure the successful completion of the entire CNC process from design to finished product.

- **User Guide:** Learn how to use NestStudio through step-by-step tutorials covering essential operations such as connecting to the device, importing files, and setting up projects.
- **Categorized Learning:** Access learning materials designed for beginners and users who want to explore CNC machining workflows and best practices in greater detail. Topics include one-click smart milling parameter settings, workpiece reclamping during machining, process tree configuration, and simulation previews.
