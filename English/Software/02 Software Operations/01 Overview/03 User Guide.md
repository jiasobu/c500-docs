### User Guide


Users can access the **User Guide** through the left menu bar on the Home page to master the core operations of NestStudio, obtain professional tool selection advice, and learn CNC machining knowledge to ensure the successful completion of the entire CNC process from design to finished product.


- **User Guide:** Users can learn quick operation tutorials for NestStudio, including remote control and monitoring of equipment, selecting appropriate tools based on items, and correct material clamping methods.

- **Categorized Learning:** Provides learning content for novice users or those wishing to understand CNC machining standard processes, including: how to align stock, how to align toolpaths, laser outlining operations, automatic centering methods for flat and curved stock, equipment calibration processes, and common troubleshooting such as device connection failure or file transmission failure.
