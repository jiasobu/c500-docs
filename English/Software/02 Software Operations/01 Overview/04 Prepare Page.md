### Prepare Page


Click **Prepare** to enter the ready interface, where users can precisely adjust parameters related to model size, material selection, and process settings.

![](https://docs.worksbase.com/neststudio/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87/preparepage-en.png)<!-- publication-image: {width=12cm} -->



| **No.** | **Feature**                          | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| :------ | :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1       | **Top Toolbar**                    | Includes Home, File Menu, Open file, Settings, Help, Messages, Account Login, Minimize, and Close software; Prepare page, Preview page, and Device page.                                            |
| 2       | **Project Files**                   | Lists currently created projects and the project being operated on; allows for quick creation of new projects.                                                                                                    |
| 3       | **Connect Device**           | 1. Displays the current device connection status.<br>2. If the device is not connected, click **Disconnected** and select a wired or wireless connection method.                                                          |
| 4       | **Process Tree**             | Level 1: Project.<br>Level 2: Model, Material, Fixture, Machining Surface Settings.<br>Level 3: Operations (divided into primary and secondary operations).                          |
| 5       | **Worktable**                       | Displays the machining worktable view.                                                                                                                                                      |
| 6       | **Material Size**                    | Displays the material view.                                                                                                                                        |
| 7       | **Fixture** | Renders the corresponding fixture model.                                                                                                                                |
| 8       | **View Cube**                | Controls the view angle.                                                                                                   |
| 9       | **Create Toolpath**                  | Click **Create Toolpath** to generate and preview the toolpath for the model.                                                                                                                                        |
