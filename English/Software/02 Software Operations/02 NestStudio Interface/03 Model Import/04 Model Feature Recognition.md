#### Model Feature Recognition

After model import, the system automatically evaluates its machinability, identifies geometric features such as planar surfaces, holes, pockets, slots, contours, bosses, islands, chamfers, and curved surfaces, and generates a manufacturability analysis report.

By reviewing the report, users can identify potential machining risks in advance, obtain corresponding recommendations, and optimize machining strategies to improve machining safety and accuracy.
