### Simulation Preview

After generating the toolpath, use Simulation Preview to inspect the toolpath and machining process. The machining risk report can also be reviewed to identify potential issues in advance and optimize the machining strategy.


### Simulation Preview Access

- Process Tree: Right-click the machining face setting node in the process tree and select **Simulation Preview**.
- Top Toolbar: After generating the toolpath, click **Preview** in the top toolbar.

![](https://docs.worksbase.com/neststudio/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87/simulation-preview1-en.png)<!-- publication-image: {width=12cm} -->


### Simulation Preview Playback

The simulation plays back in operation sequence. The corresponding operation is highlighted in the process tree on the left, making it easy to identify the current machining step.

![](https://docs.worksbase.com/neststudio/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87/simulation-preview2-en.png)<!-- publication-image: {width=12cm} --> 



| **No.**  | **Function**   | **Discription**                                   |
| :--- | :---- | :------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Multi-surface Toolpath Preview | Supports switching between machining surfaces (Top/Bottom) to view toolpaths and check the toolpath planning for different surfaces.  |
| 2   | Settings | Turn on **Material Removal** to simulate material removal during machining and preview the final result.      |
| 3   | Color | The operation colors in the simulation preview match the color labels of the corresponding operations in the Process Tree on the left, making it easier to distinguish between different operations. |
| 4   | Toolpath Check Report | The toolpath check report is displayed on the right. It supports checking for risks such as collisions, over-cuts, or under-cuts in the toolpath, assisting in evaluating toolpath safety and the precision of the machining results. |
