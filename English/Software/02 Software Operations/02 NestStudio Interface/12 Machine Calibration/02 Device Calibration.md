#### Device Calibration

Allows precision calibration and parameter adjustment to correct positioning deviations and improve machining safety and accuracy.

![](https://docs.worksbase.com/neststudio/%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87/device-machinecalibration-devicecalibration-en.png)<!-- publication-image: {width=12cm} -->


**Device Diagnostics**

| **Function**   | **Description**                                   |
| :---- | :------------------------------------ |
| Safety & Protection | Enable **Safety Mode** to activate the device's safety protection functions. It is recommended to keep this mode enabled during machining.  |
| Processing Assistance | Controls functions such as air purification, vacuum suction, spindle cleaning, coolant, and worktable cleaning. <br>When enabled, it helps improve chip removal and cooling performance.         |
| Lighting & Vision | Controls lighting and framing laser functions for workpiece inspection and positioning verification. |
| Mechanisms & Tools | Control the tool magazine door and reset its opening angle.<br>Tool Magazine Door Angle Reset: Automatically calibrates the tool magazine position to compensate for deviations caused by long-term use, helping prevent issues such as incomplete door closure. |



**Device Calibration**

- **Probe**: Manually adjust the probe's up and down positions and correct probe positioning errors to improve workpiece centering accuracy.

  > [!caution] Caution
  > 
  > Do not touch the workpiece or probe during centering and calibration.

- **L-Shaped Locating Fixture**: Calibrate the L-shaped locating fixture before centering. Ensure the worktable is clear of workpieces during calibration.
