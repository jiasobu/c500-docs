## NestStudio Interface

The NestStudio consists of four main modules: **Home**, **Prepare**, **Preview**, and **Devices**. The **Prepare** module includes 3-Axis Standard Part Machining, 3-Axis Relief Machining, and 4-Axis Rotary Relief Machining.

- **3-Axis Standard Part Machining**: Designed for machining standard parts in STEP/STP formats, with one face machined at a time and the spindle moving along the X, Y, and Z axes. Supports cubic stock only. Offers fast machining and a simple, easy-to-learn workflow.
- **3-Axis Relief Machining**: Designed for machining standard parts in STL/STEP formats, with one face machined at a time and the spindle moving along the X, Y, and Z axes. Supports cubic stock only. Offers fast machining and a simple, easy-to-learn workflow.
- **4-Axis Rotary Relief Machining**: Designed for machining multi-sided relief models without repeated workpiece clamping. Multiple faces can be machined in a single setup, with the A-axis rotating the workpiece for relief machining. Supports cubic and cylindrical stock. Requires the 4th-axis assembly.
